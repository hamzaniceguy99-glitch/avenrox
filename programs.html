The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 12 weeks, built on your stroke video and the data your monitor already records.

Program 01 · Sequence and ratio

The stroke

We rebuild the stroke from the catch: shins vertical, weight on, legs driving before the back opens. Then ratio — the recovery should take twice as long as the drive — and the damper setting, which most beginners have far too high.

  • 10 weeks
  • Any level
  • 3 sessions a week
  • A rowing machine

Start this program

Weeks 1–3 · Sequence

Legs, body, arms, in that order, every stroke.

Weeks 4–6 · Recovery

Ratio, and stopping the rush up the slide.

Weeks 7–8 · The catch

Connecting without a shock through the back.

Weeks 9–10 · Under fatigue

Holding form when it gets hard.

Weeks 1–3 · Base

Easy rowing, which should feel too easy.

Weeks 4–7 · Intervals

Hard work, correctly placed.

Weeks 8–10 · Build

Adding volume without breaking.

Weeks 11–12 · Sharpen

Bringing form together for a test.

Program 02 · Three or four hours a week

Training that fits

Most self-coached rowers train everything at the same uncomfortable pace. We set genuinely easy steady state, real intervals, and a weekly structure that fits your schedule rather than a college training plan.

  • 12 weeks
  • Any level
  • 3 to 5 sessions
  • A monitor with data

Start this program

Program 03 · 2k without blowing up

Pacing & testing

The 2k is a brutal test of pacing as much as fitness. We build a pacing plan, practice it in pieces, and put a proper test at the end — then use the result to reset training zones rather than to feel bad about a number.

  • 8 weeks
  • Rowing regularly
  • 4 sessions a week
  • A recent test, if any

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · Hinge

Weeks 3–4 · Mobility

Ankles and hamstrings, which limit the catch.

Weeks 5–6 · Core

Stability under load.

Weeks 7–8 · Integration

Strength work that shows up in the stroke.

Program 04 · Rowing for years

Strength & back health

Rowing rewards strong hips and a stable trunk. We build general strength and mobility for rowers with limited equipment, focused on the hinge pattern and the hamstring and ankle mobility that make a good catch possible. Pain goes to a clinician, not to us.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 sessions a week
  • Floor space
