How we work

Coaching, not physical therapy

Rowing is one of the few pieces of gym equipment that will happily let you do the same wrong thing ten thousand times. Technique coaching is worth more here than almost anywhere else.

What we don't do is treat anything. Back pain, shoulder pain and any medical condition belong with a healthcare professional first. We'll adapt training around what they tell you, and we won't guess about symptoms over video.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

What damper setting should I use?

Usually lower than you have it — somewhere around 3 to 5 for most people. It is a gearing setting, not a difficulty setting, and we'll explain what it actually does.

My lower back hurts when I row.

Stop and see a clinician. Then, once you're cleared, it is very often a sequence problem — opening the back before the legs finish — and that is fixable.

Is rowing good for weight loss?

It burns calories like any hard cardio, and we make no promises about weight. Our targets are technique, fitness and splits.

Do I need a Concept2?

No, though data helps. Any machine with a monitor works, and we'll tell you what your machine's numbers do and don't mean.

How often should I row?

Three sessions a week produces real progress if they're structured. More helps only if most of it is genuinely easy.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
