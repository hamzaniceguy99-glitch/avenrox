Why the erg hurts your back

Because the back is doing a job the legs were supposed to start.

How we work

Sequence, ratio, patience

The stroke has an order: legs drive, body swings, arms finish, and the reverse coming back. Break the order and the lumbar spine absorbs the load that the quads and glutes should have taken.

Ratio is the second problem. Rushing the recovery raises stroke rate, wrecks the catch and makes everything feel harder for a worse split.

Training then has to be mostly easy. Rowers who train every session at a moderate burn plateau quickly; adding genuinely easy volume and a small amount of hard work is what moves the numbers.
